2Inspections

Thursday morning, November 19: John will conduct the general home inspection, and we'll also have the sewer scope done.

You'll get an email confirmation from John soon with the details.

I'm not recommending a structural engineer right away. Let's hear John's assessment first. If he identifies something that needs a specialist, we'll decide on an engineer then.

Inspection objection: Wednesday, November 25. This is our opportunity to request repairs, credits, or concessions based on the findings. If we object, we negotiate with the seller. The resolution deadline is Tuesday, December 1. If we cannot reach agreement by then, the contract can terminate unless you withdraw the objection and move forward as-is; a proper contract termination generally provides for return of earnest money.

Holiday heads-up: November 26 is Thanksgiving. We'll aim to review findings and choose our approach well before then.

3Homeowners insurance

Do this early. Your insurance termination deadline is Wednesday, December 2, but I want you to start getting quotes and reviewing property history right away.

You'll need to contact an insurance provider yourself. Ask about premiums, coverage, deductibles, eligibility, and any prior claims history that the insurer can share. Insurance information can sometimes surface past issues worth investigating.

Under this contract, you can terminate in your sole subjective discretion if insurance availability, terms, conditions, or premium are unsatisfactory, provided timely written notice is delivered. It's an important protection, so please don't leave it to the last minute.
